Frequently asked questions

Good questions.

Who can join YARN?

Adults aged 18 and above. Age is confirmed through identity verification.

Do I need to be wealthy or established to join?

No. Verification is optional and has pathways for students, graduates, employees and business owners. Income does not decide your worth.

Are my documents visible to other members?

Never. Others only see which verification badges you have earned.

Does verification guarantee someone is safe?

No. It reduces risk but cannot remove it. We encourage first dates in public places.

Is YARN free?

Pricing has not been decided yet. We will be clear about any costs before you pay for anything.
