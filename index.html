Got Questions?

Good questions. Honest answers.

Who can join YARN?

Adults aged 18 and above who want genuine, intentional relationships. Whether you're looking for a committed relationship or marriage, YARN is for people who value honesty.

What makes YARN different from Tinder or Bumble?

Unlike rapid swiping apps designed to keep you addicted, YARN centers around intent transparency, multi-tiered profile verification, and structured limits that prioritize deep compatibility over endless superficial swipes.

Are my verification documents visible to other members?

Never. Others only see the verified badge checkmark on your profile. Your identification documents are encrypted and kept strictly confidential.

Does verification guarantee someone is safe?

Verification reduces risk significantly by eliminating catfishes, but cannot remove every human risk. We always encourage first dates in busy public spaces.

Is YARN free to join?

Creating your profile, completing basic verification, and meeting matches is free. Premium features that enhance your experience will always be transparent before any payment.
